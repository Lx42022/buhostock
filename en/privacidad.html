Privacy policy

Version of 23 October 2026 · This translation is for information only; the Spanish version is binding.

1. Who processes your data

BuhoStock. You can write to us about any privacy matter at buhostock.team@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

Only what you give us in the “Try it free for 30 days” form: your name or your shop's name, your e-mail, your Wallapop profile, the approximate number of listings and, if you like, your WhatsApp. We also store the date of sign-up and the version of the terms you accepted.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics tools and no advertising. The fonts are hosted on our own server.

3. What we use it for

  • To contact you and prepare your free trial. Legal basis: your request, as a step prior to a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To send you BuhoStock news by e-mail, only if you tick the box and confirm it in the e-mail we send you (double opt-in). Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 21 LSSI-CE). You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell your data or use it for anything else.

4. How long we keep it

If you don't become a customer, we delete your data 12 months after signing up. Data for news is kept until you unsubscribe. If you become a customer, for as long as the service lasts and afterwards for the periods required by law.

5. Who else sees it

We use these providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google (form, spreadsheet and e-mail).
  • GitHub (hosting of this website; it logs technical data such as the IP address in order to serve the pages).

Both may process data outside the EU, in the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to buhostock.team@gmail.com. We will reply within one month at most.

If you think we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version here with its date.
